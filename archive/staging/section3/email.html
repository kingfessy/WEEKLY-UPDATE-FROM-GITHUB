LIQUIDITY AND CREDIT

Week ending October 22, 2026
Quick Summary

Funding rates edged lower while bank credit expanded in early October. SOFR slipped to 3.87 percent and EFFR to 3.88 percent, hinting at cheaper short-term funding. The IORB remained steady at 3.9 percent, keeping the policy stance unchanged. Total bank credit rose to $19.95T and commercial loans to $2.96T, indicating more lending capacity. The DVP Repo Rate held at 3.89 percent, reinforcing a stable repo market. Overall, conditions appear to be loosening slightly.
