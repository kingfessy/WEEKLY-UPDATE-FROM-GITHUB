Quick Summary

Funding rates edged lower this week, hinting at modest easing. The drop in SOFR and DVP Repo Rate each by 0.01 percentage points reduces the cost of short-term borrowing. Deposit growth at both large and small banks supports liquidity in the system. On balance, the data point to marginally looser funding conditions.
