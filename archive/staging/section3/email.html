LIQUIDITY AND CREDIT

Week ending November 13, 2026
Quick Summary

Funding conditions show a hint of easing as benchmark rates drift lower. SOFR slipped by seven basis points while the tri-party repo rate edged down. Total bank credit nudged higher, adding roughly ten billion dollars of new lending. DVP repo rates inched up, offering a small counterbalance. Overall the mix points to marginally looser liquidity in the system.

SOFR

2026-11-09

SOFR slipped to 3.88 percent on 2026-11-09.

That is a 0.07 percentage point decline from the prior reading.

The drop points to a modest easing of short-term funding pressure.

EFFR

2026-11-09

EFFR held at 3.88 percent on 2026-11-09.

The rate was unchanged from the previous measurement.

Stability suggests funding costs remain flat for overnight borrowing.

IORB

2026-11-09

IORB stayed at 3.9 percent on 2026-11-09.

There was no change from the prior period.

This steadiness indicates no new strain on inter-bank borrowing rates.

Total Bank Credit

2026-10-28

That represents an increase of $9.5 billion from the prior period.

The growth signals expanding credit availability across the banking sector.

DVP Repo Rate

Other Updates

GCF Repo Rate slipped to 3.93 percent, Treasury General Account fell to $914.4 billion, and Overnight Reverse Repo dropped to $0.3 billion.
