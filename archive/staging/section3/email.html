LIQUIDITY AND CREDIT

Week ending November 06, 2026
Quick Summary

Funding conditions show mixed signals this week. Overnight rates are largely steady with SOFR up to 3.95 percent and EFFR unchanged at 3.88 percent. Repo rates are nudging lower, DVP Repo fell to 3.96 percent while Tri-Party and GCF repos edged higher to 3.95 and 3.99 percent. Total bank credit expanded to $20.01 trillion and commercial loans rose to $2.96 trillion, indicating more lending capacity. At the same time large and small bank deposits slipped and total bank assets contracted, pointing to some pressure on liquidity. Overall the picture is neither clearly tighter nor looser but a balance of modest easing in rates and modest growth in credit.

What to Watch
